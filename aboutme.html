About Me

Hello, my name is Yeilanny. I am a 21 y/o digital artist from New Mexico who also dabbles in 3D modeling!

I have been drawing digitally for 10 years, using Clip Studio Paint for every step in my artwork process.

I am currently working on a few 2.5D projects that combine 2D and 3D elements. I enjoy media that displays animation and modeling, and will begin to take commissions soon!

Some of my current interests are Splatoon, Great God Grove, Smile For Me, The Elder Scrolls, and the ENA series!
